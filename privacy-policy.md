# Privacy Policy — Colonaut

_Last updated: 2026-11-15_

Colonaut ("the game") is a mobile game published by **Oleksandr Mikheienkov** ("we").
This page explains what data the game uses. Contact: **oleksandr.mikheienkov@gmail.com**.

## Short version

- The game has no accounts and we run no servers. We do not collect, store or sell your
  personal data ourselves.
- The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect data (such as the advertising
  ID) to show and measure ads.
- Your progress is saved only on your phone.

## Data stored on your device

Records, stars, rockets, campaign progress and settings are saved locally on your phone.
They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If device backup is enabled
in Android, this data may be included in your own Google backup.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, full-screen and rewarded ads.
Google may collect and process:

- the advertising ID of your device,
- IP address (which gives an approximate location),
- device and app information (model, OS version, language),
- how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data.

This data is used to show ads (personalised only if you agree), limit how often you see an
ad, measure ads and prevent fraud. Details:
[How Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Your choices

- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ad is
  loaded (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time:
  **Settings → Privacy settings** in the game.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings
  (Settings → Google → Ads).

## Playing with a friend nearby

The optional "Duel" mode connects two phones directly through Bluetooth and Wi-Fi using
Google Nearby Connections. Android requires the "Nearby devices" permission for this (on
Android 12 and older: the Location permission). The game uses it only to find the other
phone. It does not read, store or send your location. During a duel the two phones exchange
only game data (rocket position, distance, chosen rocket) and a random name such as
"Colonaut-1234". Nothing goes to a server.

A "ghost challenge" is a text code that you choose to share yourself. It contains only the
recording of your run (level seed, tap timing, distance, rocket).

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data
from children.
